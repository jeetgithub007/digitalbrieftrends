How It Works

From Raw Data to Actionable Insights in 4 Steps

Fetch

7 fetchers run in parallel, pulling headlines, trends, and search data from Google Trends, news APIs, RSS feeds, and Reddit simultaneously.

Aggregate

All articles merge into one stream. Duplicates detected at 78% title similarity are combined. Multi-source stories earn bonus points.

Score & Rank

Every trend receives a composite score (0-100) weighted by source authority, social signals, search volume, and cross-validation.

Deliver

Ranked trends flow to the live dashboard, REST API, and content idea generator. Ready for your website, newsletter, or editorial workflow.
