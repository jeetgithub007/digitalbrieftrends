1. Overview

Welcome to DigitalBrief (operated by DigitalBrief & Jeet Digital Marketing Agency). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of users accessing our multi-source intelligence platform, news aggregation pipeline, and editorial publication services.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when you visit our website (digitalbrief.in) or interact with our REST APIs.

🔒 Key Commitment: We do not sell personal identification data to third-party brokers. All data processing strictly serves the delivery of real-time trend intelligence and personalized user preferences.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users. The categories of information collected include:

  • Usage & Device Telemetry: Log data such as IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and page access timestamps.
  • Saved Preferences: Saved or dismissed trend bookmarks stored locally in server session memory or web browser state.
  • Newsletter Communications: Email address provided voluntarily when subscribing to the DigitalBrief Daily Digest.

3. AI Pipeline Data Processing

DigitalBrief aggregates trend topics from public RSS feeds, search trend signals, and verified news APIs. Our automated enrichment engine processes public news headlines and summaries to compute velocity signals, sentiment metrics, and factual article summaries.

No private personal communication or user-generated private data is ingested into our public trend scoring models.

4. Cookies & Telemetry

We use essential cookies and browser local storage to maintain session preferences, save bookmark states, and monitor API performance. You can control cookie preferences directly through your browser settings.

5. Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information with external organizations, except in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly request service integration.
  • Legal Requirements: To comply with applicable laws, government regulations, or legal processes.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data stored with us.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy inquiries regarding this policy, please reach out via our Contact Page or email privacy@digitalbrief.in.
