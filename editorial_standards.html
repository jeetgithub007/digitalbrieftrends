1. Editorial Mission

DigitalBrief aims to provide business leaders, developers, and tech strategists with clear, objective, and timely technology trend intelligence. We combine human editorial oversight with automated multi-source news signals to filter noise and highlight high-impact developments across AI, startups, cybersecurity, and global markets.

💡 Core Principles: Accuracy, Transparency, Independence, and Factual Clarity over Clickbait.

2. Multi-Source Verification

To eliminate single-source bias or false rumors, our trend pipeline scores story urgency across 140+ verified feeds (including Reuters, TechCrunch, Economic Times, Bloomberg, ISRO releases, and CISA advisories). Stories are validated by verifying cross-platform coverage and source authority weights.

3. Responsible AI & Transparency

We use AI tools to generate structured executive summaries, extract key takeaways, and translate technical jargon into actionable insights. Our AI policy mandates:

  • Factual Grounding: AI summaries must strictly reflect verified primary source reporting. Fabricated claims or unverified rumors are flagged and filtered.
  • Human Editorial Review: Articles published to our primary editorial catalog undergo human review before final syndication.
  • No AI Deception: AI-assisted summaries are clearly labeled as synthesized intelligence.

4. Source Attribution

Every story card and article published on DigitalBrief explicitly displays the original news source, publisher badge, and direct link to the original reporting site. We respect content licensing and never bypass paywalls or full-text licensing barriers.

5. Corrections & Retractions Policy

If an error of fact is identified in any published brief, our editorial desk updates the story promptly with a clear correction note detailing what was changed and when.

To submit a factual correction, please contact our desk at Contact Page or email editor@digitalbrief.in.
